Experience

My work so far spans information technology, rural banking and real estate operations. Each role has taught me something about serving people well, whether they are students, customers, landlords or tenants.

IT Specialist Intern

Accra Technical University · Accra, Ghana ·

I worked within the university's IT department, supporting the staff and students who depend on reliable computers, network access and digital systems to carry out their academic and administrative work. The role gave me a close view of how a busy institution keeps its technology running day to day.

  • Provided first-line technical support to staff and students, diagnosing and resolving hardware, software and login problems promptly and courteously.
  • Assisted with the setup, configuration and routine maintenance of computers and peripherals across administrative offices and computer laboratories.
  • Helped monitor network connectivity and reported faults early, so that users could stay online with as little disruption as possible.
  • Supported user account administration, including account creation, password resets and access permissions for institutional systems.
  • Documented recurring issues and their solutions, building a practical reference that made future support work faster and more consistent.
  • Promoted basic cybersecurity practices among users, such as keeping software updated, running antivirus checks and choosing strong passwords.
  • Learned to explain technical problems in plain language to colleagues without a technical background, a skill I now use in every role.

Real Estate Operations

Danith Properties and Management Services Ltd. · Tse Addo, Ghana

At Danith Properties I help run the day-to-day operations of a property management agency, bringing together documentation, client onboarding and sales structure so that landlords, tenants and staff all know where they stand.

  • Drafting and managing property management agreements and client onboarding documents.
  • Preparing Ghana-specific tenancy agreements that protect both landlords and tenants.
  • Structuring the sales team and its weekly routines to keep targets, follow-ups and reporting on track.

Intern
